A cent is a hundredth of a fret's worth of pitch — 100 cents = 1 fret. Under 15 cents is close enough for rock.

How to tune up
  1. Play one open string and let it ring.
  2. Read the big note name — it tells you which string it thinks you're on.
  3. Turn the peg the way the app says until the needle sits in the green.
  4. Work through B E A D G; each one ticks off as it lands.

Press Direct Monitor on the Scarlett and use headphones — you'll hear yourself with no delay at all.
